## 1. Scope and story (decided 2026-10-15)

**G0 → CGM at fixed tile-level pairing** (consensus over the 5 scanner views of a SCORPION
tile): with tile-level paired data, does a consensus generative model trained through the
frozen backbone (CGM, Exp 7) improve over its linear limit G0 on PLISM, while keeping HEST?
Inference is one global map on one H0-mini GAP embedding: no scanner ID, no paired view.

- Rows in the paper: raw H0-mini GAP; the published eraser (slide-level pairing, λ=0.5) as the
  external baseline; G0 (closed-form consensus shrinkage, CGM's linear limit); the eraser at
  tile level (same pairing unit, objective control); CGM V0-S (linear encoder, ablation); CGM
  V1-S (selected arm, three seeds 7b71 / 8ed0 / ac94). Ablations in the text or supplement: V2-S, no
  augmentation, untrained init, LOSO.
- Framing: most of the PLISM gain over G0 is already in the *trained linear* arm V0-S; the
  nonlinear encoder adds a small, consistent amount. The contribution is the generative
  training (per-scanner likelihood, ELBO through the backbone), the nonlinearity is an
  ablation. The per-scanner likelihood is a small part: the exchangeable linear V0 is
  −0.003 below V0-S and +0.022 over G0 (claim 6), so most of the gain is training an
  exchangeable model through the backbone. The exchangeable nonlinear V1 gives the same
  picture for the selected arm: −0.001 below V1-S, +0.027 over G0 (claim 6).
- Decisions behind the scope (evidence in `docs/experiment-log.md`, section 1): D0 eraser =
  bar to beat (2026-10-13); D3 no Gaussian mixtures (2026-10-14); D2 pairing fixed at tile
  level (2026-10-15); D4 no quadratic correction (2026-10-15). D5/D6 not taken.
- Out of scope, re-evaluated in the follow-up paper in a broader setup: pairing scale
  (region / tile erasers; the region eraser is the strongest linear map on PLISM), quadratic
  correction, Gaussian mixtures, PairedVAE, Exp 5/6.
- Report order: SCORPION val (selection) → SCORPION test → PLISM → HEST. PLISM: full tables as
  the main result, the without-HR tables alongside with the reason (`docs/findings.md`, HR).
- PLISM metric in the paper (decision 2026-10-21, user): mean top-1 over slide pairs, as in
  the claims and the per-pair intervals (blocks `plism`, `plism_pairs`). Median top-1 is at
  the floor for weak backbones (UNI2-h raw, all pairs: median 0.005 vs mean 0.043). The
  plismbench leaderboard metrics (medians of cosine (all) and top-10 per shift group, plus
  their mean; blocks `plism_leaderboard`, which reproduce the README rows of H0-Mini and
  UNI2-h exactly) are kept for comparability with the leaderboard; no paired intervals on
  them. Official H0-Mini (0.541) is the CLS token; our raw row is GAP (0.709).
